# MM1030 Brand Guidelines
**Mission to Conquer 10 Malaysian Towers & 30 World's Tallest Towers**

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### Mission Statement
**Misi Menawan 10 Menara Malaysia & 30 Menara Tertinggi Dunia (MM1030)** represents an extraordinary humanitarian mission to conquer Malaysia's 10 tallest towers and the world's 30 tallest towers, promoting world peace, Malaysian excellence, and athletic achievement on the global stage.

### Brand Tagline
**"1 World 1 Hope"**

### Brand Values
- **Perseverance**: Overcoming physical and mental challenges
- **National Pride**: Representing Malaysia on the world stage
- **Peace**: Promoting global unity and humanitarian causes
- **Excellence**: Achieving extraordinary athletic feats
- **Determination**: One tower at a time, one goal in mind

---

## 2. Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
The MM1030 logo features a transparent design that combines:
- Bold, uppercase lettering for "MM1030"
- Clean, modern typography that conveys strength and ambition
- Optional tagline integration: "Misi Menawan 10 Menara Malaysia & 30 Menara Tertinggi Dunia"

### Logo Variations
- **Full Logo**: MM1030 with full mission description
- **Short Logo**: MM1030 with "1 World 1 Hope"
- **Icon Only**: MM1030 lettermark for small applications
- **Transparent Background**: For overlay on imagery

### Clear Space
Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" in MM1030 on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px width minimum
- Print: 25mm width minimum

### Logo Don'ts
- Do not stretch or distort the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside approved palette
- Do not add effects (shadows, glows, gradients)
- Do not rotate the logo
- Do not place on busy backgrounds without proper contrast

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary Colors

**Bold Red** (Courage & Determination)
- HEX: #C41E3A
- RGB: 196, 30, 58
- CMYK: 0, 85, 70, 23
- Usage: Primary brand color, call-to-action elements, headers

**Deep Navy Blue** (Trust & Ambition)
- HEX: #003366
- RGB: 0, 51, 102
- CMYK: 100, 50, 0, 60
- Usage: Text, backgrounds, secondary elements

**Gold** (Achievement & Excellence)
- HEX: #D4AF37
- RGB: 212, 175, 55
- CMYK: 0, 17, 74, 17
- Usage: Accents, achievements, awards, highlights

### Secondary Colors

**Sky Blue** (Hope & Aspiration)
- HEX: #4A90E2
- RGB: 74, 144, 226
- CMYK: 67, 36, 0, 11
- Usage: Supporting graphics, backgrounds

**White**
- HEX: #FFFFFF
- RGB: 255, 255, 255
- Usage: Text on dark backgrounds, clean space

**Charcoal Gray**
- HEX: #333333
- RGB: 51, 51, 51
- CMYK: 0, 0, 0, 80
- Usage: Body text, secondary information

### Color Psychology
- **Red**: Represents the intensity, passion, and courage required to climb the world's tallest towers
- **Navy Blue**: Symbolizes trust, professionalism, and the vast sky that connects all towers
- **Gold**: Represents achievement, excellence, and the summit of success
- **Sky Blue**: Evokes hope, aspiration, and the Malaysian spirit

## 4. Typography

### Primary Typeface: **Montserrat Bold**
- Usage: Headlines, logos, impactful statements
- Characteristics: Strong, modern, athletic
- Weights: Bold (700), ExtraBold (800), Black (900)

### Secondary Typeface: **Open Sans**
- Usage: Body text, descriptions, long-form content
- Characteristics: Clean, readable, professional
- Weights: Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)

### Malay Language Typography
For Bahasa Malaysia content, ensure proper diacritical marks and maintain the same typeface families for consistency.

### Typography Hierarchy
- **H1**: Montserrat Bold, 48-60pt
- **H2**: Montserrat Bold, 36-42pt
- **H3**: Montserrat SemiBold, 24-30pt
- **Body**: Open Sans Regular, 14-16pt
- **Caption**: Open Sans Regular, 10-12pt

## 5. Visual Identity

### Photography Style

**Tower Photography**
- Dramatic angles looking upward
- Emphasis on height and scale
- Golden hour lighting preferred
- Include human element for scale
- Both interior stairwells and exterior facades

**Action Photography**
- Athletes in motion climbing stairs
- Close-ups showing determination and effort
- Wide shots showing the tower environment
- Peak moments of achievement at summits

**Documentary Style**
- Behind-the-scenes preparation
- Team interactions
- Cultural elements from each country
- Authentic, unposed moments

### Image Treatment
- High contrast for dramatic effect
- Slightly desaturated for serious tone, or vibrant for achievement moments
- Can overlay with brand colors (red, navy, gold) at 20-30% opacity
- Vignette effect acceptable for focus

### Graphic Elements

**Tower Silhouettes**
- Simplified outlines of iconic towers
- Used as background elements
- Can be stacked to show progress

**Achievement Markers**
- Checkmarks or flags for conquered towers
- Progress bars showing mission completion
- Numbers highlighting statistics (10 towers, 30 towers)

**Malaysian Elements**
- Jalur Gemilang (Malaysian flag) colors used respectfully
- Traditional patterns as subtle backgrounds
- National emblems when appropriate

**Patterns**
- Diagonal lines suggesting stairs/ascent
- Grid patterns representing tower structures
- Topographic-style lines showing elevation

## 6. Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Inspirational**: Motivating others to push boundaries
- **Determined**: Unwavering commitment to the mission
- **Respectful**: Honoring each tower, country, and culture
- **Humble**: Acknowledging the challenge while remaining grounded
- **Proud**: Celebrating Malaysian achievement

### Tone Guidelines

**For Social Media**: Energetic, engaging, behind-the-scenes
- Use exclamation marks sparingly
- Share journey updates with authentic emotion
- Encourage community support

**For Official Communications**: Professional, respectful, informative
- Formal Malay and English
- Detailed mission updates
- Acknowledge sponsors and supporters

**For Humanitarian Content**: Compassionate, purposeful, meaningful
- Connect tower climbing to larger causes
- Highlight "1 World 1 Hope" message
- Emphasize unity and peace

### Language Usage
- **Bilingual**: Malay and English (English translations always provided)
- **Hashtags**: #MM1030 #1World1Hope #MisiMenawan
- **Avoid**: Overly technical climbing jargon without explanation

## 9. Social Media Guidelines

### Profile Setup
- **Profile Photo**: MM1030 logo on brand-colored background
- **Cover Photo**: Dramatic tower imagery with "1 World 1 Hope"
- **Bio**: Mission statement + website link + current status

### Content Pillars
1. **Journey Updates** (40%): Current tower, preparation, climb day
2. **Behind-the-Scenes** (25%): Training, team, travel
3. **Inspiration** (20%): Motivational messages, quotes
4. **Achievement** (10%): Tower completion celebrations
5. **Humanitarian** (5%): Peace message, charity connections

### Posting Frequency
- **During Active Climb**: Daily updates
- **Between Climbs**: 3-4 times per week
- **Off-Season**: 2-3 times per week

### Content Formats
- Photos: High-quality, on-brand imagery
- Videos: Climb highlights, team messages, tower reveals
- Stories: Real-time updates, polls, Q&As
- Reels/Shorts: Dynamic climbing moments, achievements
