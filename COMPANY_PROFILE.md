# 3. ABOUT SUWAIBAH NASIR

## Profile

**Name:** Dr. Suwaibah Muhammad Nasir  
**Title:** Founder & Lead Athlete, MM1030  
**Rank (Former):** Major (K), Royal Malaysian Air Force (RMAF/TUDM)  
**Specialty:** Ultra-endurance Running, Vertical Tower Running  
**Notable Achievement:** First Malaysian woman to solo run across the Sahara Desert

---

## Biography

Dr. Suwaibah Muhammad Nasir is a pioneering Malaysian ultra-endurance athlete who has redefined the boundaries of human potential through her extraordinary achievements in long-distance and vertical running.

Born and raised in Malaysia, Suwaibah developed her mental toughness and discipline through her service in the Royal Malaysian Air Force (RMAF), where she attained the rank of Major. Her military background instilled in her the values of perseverance, strategic planning, and unwavering commitment to mission success.

---

## Historic Achievements

### Sahara Desert Solo Run (2012)
**Distance:** 1,111 kilometers  
**Duration:** 32 days  
**Route:** Aswan to Port Said, Egypt  
**Significance:** First Malaysian woman to accomplish this feat

Suwaibah ran alone across one of the world's most unforgiving landscapes, battling extreme heat, sandstorms, and physical exhaustion. This journey was not merely a physical challenge but a humanitarian mission to promote peace and unity.

**Malaysia Book of Records Recognition:** Longest solo run by a Malaysian woman across a desert

---

### Across Marco Polo Run (2014)
An international ultra-endurance event following the historic Silk Road trading route, demonstrating her competitive excellence on the global stage.

---

### Silk Canal Solo Run (2016)
**Distance:** 3,333 kilometers  
**Significance:** One of the longest solo runs ever completed by a Malaysian

This extraordinary journey further cemented her reputation as one of Malaysia's most accomplished endurance athletes.

**Malaysia Book of Records Recognition:** Longest canal solo run by a Malaysian
