About Us

Our Mission

MM1030 is dedicated to promoting vertical running and tower climbing as a sport in Malaysia, inspiring youth to push beyond their limits, and establishing Malaysia as a competitive force in international stair climbing competitions.

What We Do

Youth Development

We nurture young Malaysian athletes through comprehensive training programs, mentorship, and competitive opportunities in vertical running and tower climbing.

Competitive Excellence

We prepare athletes for international competitions, including the Southeast Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, and future Olympic events.

Tower Challenges

We organize and participate in tower climbing events across Malaysia and internationally, showcasing Malaysian resilience and determination.

Community Impact

We inspire communities through sports diplomacy, promoting healthy lifestyles and demonstrating that extraordinary achievements are possible through dedication.

Our Vision

To establish Malaysia as a world-class training hub for vertical running, producing athletes who compete at the highest international levels.

We envision a future where Malaysian youth are inspired to pursue excellence in sports, equipped with world-class training facilities and guidance.

Through MM1030, we aim to build a legacy of athletic achievement that brings pride to Malaysia and demonstrates the power of human potential.

Our Core Values

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in training, competition, and character development.

Resilience

We embrace challenges as opportunities for growth and never give up on our goals.

Unity

We build a strong community of athletes, supporters, and partners united by a common vision.

Founded by a Pioneer

MM1030 was founded by Dr. Suwaibah Muhammad Nasir, the first Malaysian woman to run solo across the Sahara Desert and a multiple record holder in the Malaysia Book of Records.

Her extraordinary achievements in endurance running and vertical tower climbing inspire our mission to develop the next generation of Malaysian champions.
