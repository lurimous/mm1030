Welcome to the official MM1030 Tower Climb platform — a historic movement that unites sports, national identity, international diplomacy, and global peace advocacy under one extraordinary mission.

The MM1030 initiative is more than an athletic challenge. It is a nation-building platform, a global branding opportunity, and a symbol of Malaysia's soft power on the world stage.

At the heart of this movement stands an exceptional Malaysian, Dr. Suwaibah Muhammad Nasir, a world-renowned ultra-endurance athlete and humanitarian whose courage, discipline, and determination inspire millions.

Together, we are embarking on a mission to:

Conquer 10 iconic Malaysian towers

Followed by 30 of the world's greatest towers

Raise the Malaysian flag at every summit

Promote peace and unity through endurance sports

Advocate welfare support for Malaysian former national athletes

Position Malaysia as the Asian hub of Tower Climb sports

This is the beginning of a global legacy that elevates Malaysia's name to new heights — literally and symbolically.

A Vision Rooted in National Pride & Global Purpose

As Chairman, my commitment is to assemble the strategic pathways, partnerships, and governance that will transform MM1030 into a world-leading sports diplomacy initiative.

Our foundation is built upon:

1

A Powerful Human Story

Dr. Suwaibah's life, achievements, and endurance form the emotional and inspirational core of MM1030.

2

The Rise of Tower Climbing Sports Worldwide

Tower running is one of the fastest-growing urban sports on the planet — with global events at the Eiffel Tower, Empire State Building, Tokyo Tower, and more.

3

A Platform for Sponsors to Go Global

MM1030 offers unprecedented brand visibility: local, national, and international — spanning 40 towers across 30 countries.

4

Strategic Alliances & Global Institutions

Our collaboration with the World Federation of Great Towers (WGTF) will secure:

  • Tower accreditation
  • International listings
  • Global tourism tie-ins
  • Recognition for Malaysian landmarks
5

A National Legacy for Generations

MM1030 is designed to become:

  • A recurring annual festival
  • A youth development pathway
  • A platform for athlete welfare under YBAK
  • A global symbol of Malaysia's spirit
